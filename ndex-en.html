HOW IT WORKS

The simple process to create your custom sportswear

01

Get inspired

Tell us who you are, the values of your team and the identity you want to communicate. Every project starts from listening: colors, logos, objectives and final use are the starting point for a truly custom result.

02

Design

Our graphic team develops a personalized proposal starting from your indications. Lines, proportions and fabric rendering are studied to ensure a coherent, technical and recognizable design.

03

Final design

Once the concept is defined, we adapt the design to the selected items. Fit, materials and details are optimized to offer comfort, performance and uniformity across the entire collection.

04

Production

Once approved, the design is optimized for production. Every detail is curated to ensure the highest quality of printing and comfort, with particular attention to the durability of the garment.

Frequently Asked Questions – BL CUSTOM

Find answers to common questions about our customized services and products.

01

Bicycle Line's Custom service allows teams, clubs, companies, and events to create customized technical apparel for cycling, triathlon, athletics, and skating: from dedicated graphic design to final production.

02

Simply send a request via the contact form, indicating the discipline and approximate quantity required. Alternatively, you can write directly to custom@bicycle-line.com. Our team will provide you with an initial price estimate and, once you confirm your interest, will request your logos and artwork to properly develop your project, from the graphic design sketch to final production.

03

Yes. We can offer several ready-made graphic bases to choose from, onto which you can apply your team/club's logos and colors. In this way, we can simplify the process and help you achieve a professional result without having to develop graphics from scratch.

04

The minimum quantity is 10 pieces per item.

05

For your order to go into production, you must provide all your customer information (company name, billing details, and shipping address). Once we have collected this information, we will prepare the complete order and send it to you for confirmation: prices, quantities, sizes, and artwork. After final confirmation and receipt of the deposit, the order can be put into production.

06

Typically, after approval of the quote and artwork draft and receipt of the deposit, the standard production and delivery time is approximately 6 weeks. This may vary slightly depending on the season.

07

Yes, you can reorder with the same artwork. For reorders with the same design, the minimum quantity is 5 pieces per item.

08

When it's time to define the sizes, it will be possible to request a size sample (fit sample)

09
